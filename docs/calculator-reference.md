# Hero Team Lab calculation details and historical notes

This reference preserves the original model notes, capture history and migration details. Dated sections describe the release and account profile under study at that time. Paths to spreadsheets and captures refer to the original development workspace. See the [project guide](../README.md) for the current player workflow and the [contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for build instructions.

Open **hero-team-lab.html** in a browser. It is a standalone offline file: no installation, server, Google login, or network connection is required.

Choose Energy, **Energy + Power Mages**, Gold, Experience, Damage, **Damage · Buff swap**, or **Immortal Boss Damage**. Each focus keeps a separate **Team A and Team B**, with its own names, heroes, levels, tiles and locks. Switching focuses immediately loads that saved pair. Account calculation settings and the suggestion roster are shared. Edit the teams and compare results. Support levels default to **15,000**. Newly selected Experience levelers start at **1**. All 42 available heroes now include screenshot-derived milestones through **level 10,000**; no extra level-15,000 milestone is invented. **Suggest team B** searches permitted candidates and tile assignments while keeping locked heroes and levels. Up to **three copies of each hero** are allowed. The team search is a heuristic, not a guarantee of the best possible lineup.

**Experience suggestions require at least one Archer, Ballista and Veteran.** Gold requires Hunter, Rogue and Sovereign; Energy + Power Mages requires Captain. Ordinary Energy, Damage, Damage · Buff swap and all four Immortal modes have no XP-hero minimum. Immortal still honors the supports required by its selected buff preset. These are minimums, so extra support copies remain allowed. Required heroes stay eligible outside the optional roster. Locks and a fixed attacker remain protected; conflicting locks or too few slots produce an explanation. XP abilities remain available and contribute to XP comparisons whenever their heroes are present in another focus; those heroes are not forced into its suggestions. Only one copy supplies each XP ability; duplicate copies remain supports and do not repeat the ability.

Version-16 setups use these focus-specific requirements. The latest user correction supersedes the earlier instruction to include the XP trio in every build. Existing saved and imported teams stay intact, including any XP heroes added by an earlier suggestion; migration does not remove them automatically. Rerun **Suggest team B** in the desired focus to search with the corrected requirements. Missing required heroes are flagged only for the applicable focus.

**Damage teams each have a selected attacker.** Choose it in the team editor; the damage score uses that hero’s personal milestones and personal synergies plus the team’s global bonuses. Initially the hero with the highest current damage index is selected. Damage suggestions keep the attacker and its level by default while searching for supports and tiles, even if it is outside the suggestion roster. Enable **Choose the best attacker too** beside Suggest team B to search for attacker and support combinations together. Each candidate lineup is scored by its highest-damage member, including that member’s personal milestones and active personal synergies plus global bonuses. The winning attacker is selected automatically. Locked heroes and slot levels are retained; unlocked heroes may be replaced from the suggestion roster. Suggest tiles always keeps the currently selected attacker. Copying or swapping teams also carries the attacker selection. Removing its slot selects the strongest remaining hero. In the standard comparisons, the same attacker supplies the Damage, Gold and XP metrics. Support heroes contribute only global bonuses to its kills. Energy is wave income and does not use the attacker; its attacker selector only controls the other three comparison cards.

**Gold defaults to Mimic farming.** Each team uses its selected attacker’s personal effects, all applicable team globals and the configured active Gold skills. **Suggest team B** searches for supports at the same rate of Mimic kills. **Choose the best attacker too** also searches eligible attackers. Tile suggestions keep the selected attacker.

**Gold per Mimic** weights ordinary, Super and Ultra drops by their chances. Ultra rolls only after a successful Super roll. Excess Super chance increases the Super amount and excess Ultra chance increases the Ultra amount. A Mimic receives the ordinary Wave Boss Gold factor and a further ×3 base reward. Your +340% Mimic Gold makes the latter **×13.2**. The Wave Boss Gold setting accepts an account percentage read with no heroes deployed. A deployed +912% reading can include global synergies that Team Lab already calculates. The initial account-only setting of 0% normalizes that unknown factor. For a Crusader attacker, Golden Arrows gold from configured hits on the Mimic contributes separately.

**Gold per hour** uses your estimate of one encounter per 20 enemies. Enter each team’s enemies cleared per hour and, if measured, the percentage of encounters with two Mimics. A directly measured rate of successful Mimic kills per hour takes precedence and already includes doubles and missed kills. The +67% Mimic Spawn Chance and +55% Extra Boss Chance are represented in the observed frequency. The results are relative gold indexes. The **standard boss** option retains the earlier comparison for a guaranteed Ultra boss kill with shared chance spillover.

**Damage skills and placements:** Damage includes the selected attacker's captured active and mastery bonuses for Praetorian, Deadeye, Dicemaster, Warden and Warlord, scored **while the skill is active**. A Damage tile gives its occupant **+2,475% Damage (×25.75)**; a skill tile multiplies both active and mastery bonuses by **1.20**. The comparison table evaluates normal, milestone, synergy, skill and Damage placements for each team's attacker, with a button to apply the best legal exchange. **Suggest tiles** searches these placements together; **Suggest team B** uses the same skill-aware score for fixed or automatically selected attackers. Support heroes' personal skills do not contribute to the attacker.

The editable **Damage skills & attacker tile** profile uses the supplied no-tile screenshots. Edited distances scale proportionally from that reference pair; the rounded +1,236% per 0.07 m labels do not exactly reconstruct the observed total. Dicemaster uses an expected roll with the captured 41.2% maximum-roll mastery chance, assuming other rolls are uniform within their displayed ranges. A skill tile boosts that probability as well as the roll ranges, with probability capped at 100%. These profiles stay fixed as the lineup changes, so account, GM, event, map mastery and raw Skill Power are not applied again. This is a single-target bonus index with uncapped attack-speed factors: extra targets, range and cooldown averaging are excluded. Splash is now included as an additional single-target multiplier, including Warlord’s +1,436% active bonus. Heroes without a personal attack skill profile still receive global support buffs. ## Immortal Boss Damage

Choose **Immortal Boss Damage** and select **Skill damage**, **Melee**, **Mage**, or **Ranged**. Each rule keeps its own Team A and Team B. **Suggest team B** searches the active rule. **Suggest all four B teams** uses each rule’s saved priority and buff preset. Heroes of any class can supply buffs and synergies. **Attacks and damage by hero** shows whose attacks reach the boss and how much each hero contributes.

| Rule | Default buff team | Search priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill damage | Deploy Warlock, Wizard, Titan and Apprentice | Highest combined boss DPS from hits classified as skill damage |
| Melee | Swap in the four supports | Highest combined Lightning Storm strikes per second, then boss DPS |
| Mage | Warlock cooldown swap | Most scoring Mage hits per second, then Ultra Crit coverage |
| Ranged | Warlock cooldown swap | Most scoring Ranged hits per second, then Ultra Crit coverage |

For Mage and Ranged, **Most scoring hits per second** counts each eligible projectile, skill hit and damage tick that reaches the boss. A Hunter attacking 15 times per second with guaranteed Wizard triple shot produces 45 hits per second. Choose **Most team attacks per second** to count one attack each time an eligible hero attacks the boss. A damaging skill that replaces a shot counts once. Skymage spends some attack cycles teleporting. Both rate priorities settle ties by comparing the weakest attacking hero’s effective Ultra Crit chance, then expected Ultra hits/s, then boss DPS. **Highest total boss damage per second** ranks by damage directly.

**Attacks and damage by hero** shows which attacking heroes reach 100% effective Ultra and which fall short. Each crit-eligible hit must pass the normal, Super and Ultra rolls in order. At 100%, 80% and 90% respectively, 72% of eligible hits reach Ultra. New setups use the recorded pre-deployment account values of 135% Crit, 109% Super and 48% Ultra. Earlier setups retain their saved inputs. Choose **Use Damage pre-team chances** to copy the account chances you already saved in Damage. Under **Boss and skill settings**, you can also enter account values before deploying heroes and the active Crit, Super and Ultra attribute ranks for each hero (+1 percentage point per rank). The guarantee applies to the modeled buff conditions. Compare against in-game hero stats with the same buffs active.

To test a speed ascension, copy A to B. In **My ascensions → Team B build**, select the hero and change **Max Attack Speed**. Each rank adds 1/s to that hero’s base cap of 10/s, up to 15/s. The rank applies to every deployed copy. In **Boss and skill settings**, enter the hero’s separate **Attack Speed attribute rank** (0–10, +3% per rank) and any **additional account Attack Speed**. Both settings start at zero and apply to A and B. For account bonuses that multiply, enter the combined increase. Two additional ×1.2 and ×1.5 factors give ×1.8, or **80%**. Map, style, milestones, synergies, personal rank and configured skills already enter the model separately.

Read the pre-cap speed in **Attacks and damage by hero** to price the upgrade. A hero with 12/s before the cap can gain 2 attacks/s from raising a 10/s cap to 15/s. A hero with 9/s gains zero at that lineup’s current speed. Each 1/s of excess speed adds 10% real damage per hit before the Immortal’s logarithm. Attacks/s assumes the target is in range and the configured speed buffs apply. Check a hero whose in-game attack speed is below its cap when calibrating account bonuses. A capped reading only gives a lower bound on its speed before the cap.

The four-support deployment preset includes its named heroes even outside the optional roster. Swapped heroes contribute their lasting buffs to recipients that received them. Apprentice’s damage-taken debuff requires an eligible, deployed aura on the boss. Each Champion casts independently at the configured Warlock interval, initially one second. Multiple casting Champions are the confirmed Immortal exception to the usual duplicate-support ability rule. Locks keep deployed heroes and slot levels. Full-roster suggestions are a heuristic search.

The Immortal applies log₁₀ to each hit’s real damage, with the highest crit tier adding 10%, 25%, or 50% after the logarithm. For example, an Ultra crit with 8.25 × 10⁶⁰ real damage gives about 91.37 boss damage. Storm strikes land on one boss, with up to 100 prior strikes adding mastery damage within each cast. The two Champion captures in `source/immortal-review` give **88.5 strikes (89 in combat), 1,710% per hit and +499% mastery** with no heroes deployed, and **261 strikes, 5,160% and +813% mastery** with a team. The strike counts and damage percentages correspond to 161 and 506 effective Skill Power respectively. Grand Masters compound skill and mastery power by 1.05 per copy for this event. The skill tile scales Skill Power before strikes are rounded.

**Boss and skill settings** calibrates the modeled DPS to your account. The run-level damage curve and each hero’s recorded base damage and attack speed are included. The additional account damage exponent defaults to zero. The common pre-team Skill Power and mastery inputs for other heroes are estimates. The boss is assumed to stay in range. Per-second rates use the configured attack and skill windows. Actual damage depends on your buffs, positioning and account stats.

## Experience leveling from 1 to 4,500

In **Experience**, pick the hero to level for Team A or Team B. A newly selected hero starts at level 1 with no combat style. All deployed copies of that hero share its current level and combat style; two copies leave at most eight Grand Masters on a full team, and three leave at most seven; support heroes can use other styles. Enter its actual level if the climb is already underway. An existing teammate can become the leveler, or a new hero can fill a free slot or replace the last unlocked slot. Switching targets restores all former leveler copies to the saved support level. Choosing another copy of the same hero preserves its progression and style. The bulk level control sets support levels on both XP teams.

With Snow Fort enabled, the **XP +20%** tile multiplies its occupant’s kill reward by **1.20**. Veteran’s Exp Share passes that boosted reward to each eligible hero. For example, with guaranteed sharing, a 100 XP kill by a hero on the tile gives the leveling hero 120 XP. A leveling hero standing on the tile receives 100 XP when a teammate makes the same kill off the tile. Any one Experience slot can hold the tile, including a maxed attacker or a duplicate.

The **Experience attacker tile comparison** compares Normal, XP +20%, Milestone, Synergy and Skill power on the current killer. The displayed score is the XP received by the selected leveling hero. Each option accounts for the attacker giving up its current tile and any required exchange with another slot. Apply a recommendation to Team A or B, or use **Suggest tiles**, **Suggest team B** or the leveling plan to compare placements across the lineup. A change of leveling target keeps the tile on its existing occupant. Turning off Snow Fort disables its bonus.

**Suggest team B** searches supports and tiles at the current level. Choose **Best available** combat style and **Best on team** attacker to compare direct and shared kills, or select a particular unlocked style and attacker. The search keeps the leveler and a manually selected attacker in their slots. A separate attacker needs Veteran on the team for the leveler to earn XP.

**Plan levels 1–4,500** searches for a team, tiles, attacker and style for each range using Team B’s leveler, supports’ levels, locks and allowed roster. The plan compares all unlocked styles and all eligible attackers at each breakpoint, including when you have pinned choices for the current-level comparison. Breakpoints include new styles, eligible synergy tiers, the personal boss bonus and the chosen hero’s XP-relevant milestones. Every kill is treated as a boss kill. The table combines consecutive levels that recommend the same style, attacker, heroes and tiles. When only XP changes, one row shows the payout range. **Compare payouts** lets you inspect the boss XP at a level within that range. **Use in B** keeps the leveler’s current level if it falls inside the recommendation and otherwise starts at the range’s first level. The team search ranks XP per kill at the same kill rate. Timing the climb would also require boss kills per hour and XP costs per level.
