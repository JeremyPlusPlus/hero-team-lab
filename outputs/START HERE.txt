HERO TEAM LAB - QUICK START
Updated November 19, 2026

1. Extract this ZIP to a folder.
2. Open hero-team-lab.html in your desktop web browser.
3. Choose a focus, review the account settings, and set up Team A and Team B.
4. Use Suggest team B or Suggest tiles to compare alternatives.

The calculator runs offline. No installation, account or server is needed.
All required code, styling and hero data are inside the HTML file.

INCLUDED MODES
Energy, Energy + Power Mages, Gold, Experience, Damage, Damage - Buff swap,
and Immortal Boss Damage each keep their own team comparisons.
Gold and Damage use the selected attacker. Choose the best attacker too
lets suggestions search for the attacker as well as supports.

For Experience, select the hero to level. All copies of that hero share its
level and combat style. Plan levels 1-4,500 compares teams, styles, attackers
and tiles across the climb. Use in B loads a recommended range.
Best available compares Tycoon from level 1,250 and Magnate from 4,000,
including their global Rank EXP and synergy boosts when another hero attacks.
The plan checks Magnate for levels 4,000-4,500. You can also pin it manually.
Experience supports have their own Grand Master, Tycoon or Magnate choices.
Each team has a Compare support styles in suggestions checkbox, enabled by
default. Suggestions can then compare mixed support styles; turn it off to
keep manual styles. Locks protect heroes and levels, while styles can change.
Current comparisons use the styles shown. Copies of a hero share one style,
and all copies must reach its unlock level. The actual GM count is displayed.
Old teams keep their inherited Grand Master setup until edited or suggested.
With Snow Fort enabled, the XP tile gives the hero making the kill +20% XP.
Veteran shares that boosted reward with every eligible hero. If a teammate
kills off the tile, standing the leveling hero on it adds no XP to that kill.
The attacker tile table compares XP, Normal, Milestone, Synergy and Skill
power. Suggest tiles and the leveling plan also compare other placements.
Changing the leveling target keeps the tile on its current occupant.

For Immortal Boss Damage, choose Skill damage, Melee, Mage, or Ranged.
Suggest all four B teams searches each boss with its own buff preset.
Open Damage by hero to inspect scoring hits, Lightning Storm and boss DPS.

Required heroes depend on the focus:
- Experience: at least one Archer, Ballista and Veteran.
- Gold: at least one Hunter, Rogue and Sovereign.
- Energy + Power Mages: at least one Captain.
Energy, Damage, Buff swap and all four Immortal modes have no XP-hero minimum.
Immortal still keeps the supports required by its selected buff preset.
XP abilities still contribute to XP comparisons when present in other builds;
the XP trio is not forced into those builds.

This corrects the earlier all-build requirement. Existing saved and imported
teams are preserved, including any XP heroes added by earlier suggestions.
Rerun Suggest team B in each desired focus to get a new recommendation.

SET THE CALCULATOR TO YOUR ACCOUNT
Included defaults reflect one account, not universal game values. Review
hero levels, account bonuses, event/map options and active skill settings.

Open My ascensions and select the account baseline to enter your current
12 ranks for each hero. They save automatically and apply to every focus.
Global Damage, Kill Gold, Rank EXP and Energy Income apply per deployed copy.
To compare Hunter builds, copy A to B, select Team B build in My ascensions,
then try Kill Gold 14, Milestone 0 and Synergy 0. Team A keeps the baseline.
Empty trial fields inherit your account ranks. Enter 0 for an unpurchased
upgrade. Choose Use my baseline for this hero to clear that hero's trial.

Gold defaults to farming Mimic Boss Chests. The expected payout includes
Super and Ultra proc chances. For an hourly index, enter enemies cleared
per hour for each team. The starting estimate is one Mimic encounter per
20 enemies. Enter the share of double encounters if known. A measured
successful Mimic kills/hour takes precedence over those estimates.

The Gold chance defaults are 50% Super / 50% Ultra BEFORE deploying heroes.
Damage defaults are 135% Crit / 109% Super Crit / 48% Ultra Crit before heroes.
The Chance reference section accepts your pre-team totals or a capture of
Team A/B with an attacker and active skills. Do not enter deployed totals
as pre-team values. Each tier contributes only chance above its own 100% cap
to the shared spillover pool. Gold, Damage and Buff swap save separate references.

The Experience reference is +66,000,000 Rank EXP, about 260% Super / 450% Ultra
EXP chance on the recorded Wizard/Viking team with Archer level 5,000 and
ten Grand Masters. Replace these with your own measured totals and capture
the matching team. Other reference settings are assumptions shown in the app.
The all-Grand-Master checkbox describes the capture, not the leveling plan.

XP skill settings default to the supplied no-tile captures: Archer +13,240%
with wave-75,000 mastery and full boss-tag coverage; Ballista +3,865% plus
6.5% per mastery stack; Veteran +149.2 Super EXP chance points and +1,626%
Super EXP at 100 stacks. Default mastery stacks are 100 for warmed-up farming.
Change Ballista to 68 stacks to reproduce the screenshot's +442% mastery.
The XP tile search compares the killer's XP tile and all three
named skills with milestone/synergy tiles.
Each named ability supplies once; copies still provide milestones/synergies.
Duplicate XP heroes are support copies. Immortal remains a separate model
where multiple Champions can cast Lightning Storm, as requested.

On tagged bosses, Archer's wave mastery multiplies its whole mark. The
Skill Power tile boosts the active mark. Wave mastery and the mastery stacks
on Ballista and Veteran use account Mastery Power. With the captured Archer
profile, a killing Archer gains slightly more from XP than from Skill Power.
When another hero kills, Archer can mark from Skill Power while that killer
uses XP. Veteran shares their combined reward.
Tile predictions start from the no-tile skill values in the XP panel. Update
those values if your in-game abilities change with the lineup or level.

LEVELING RESULTS FOR THE INCLUDED XP PROFILE
These comparisons used all 42 heroes, level-15,000 supports, wave 75,000,
the synergy event, fully tagged bosses, guaranteed in-range Veteran sharing
and equal boss kill rates. The killer stands on the XP tile in every row.

Hero to level   Levels                    Boss killer    Leveler's tile
Archer          1-999                     Templar        Skill Power
                1,000-1,249               Archer         XP
                1,250-2,499               Templar        Skill Power
                2,500-4,500               Archer         XP
Ballista        1-999                     Viking         Skill Power
                1,000-4,500               Ballista       XP
Veteran         1-999                     Archer         Skill Power
                1,000-4,500               Veteran        XP

When Archer kills for Veteran, a second Archer on Skill Power supplies the
mark. At levels 1,500-2,499, Ballista's own kills led Templar's shared kills
by just 0.57% per boss. Kill speed can decide which earns more XP per hour.

Scores are comparison indexes. Searches are heuristic. Mimic farming weights
the actual chance of each payout tier. The standard boss, Damage and XP
indexes flag below-cap tiers and keep their guaranteed-tier assumption.

SAVING, UPDATING AND SHARING
Changes save in this browser when local storage is available. Keep the HTML
in the same location and use Export setup to back up your saved teams.
Back up your setup before replacing an older HTML file with this version.
Then reopen or reload the file and rerun suggestions or Rebuild plan.

Browser saves are NOT inside this ZIP. To share a particular lineup, send
its exported setup JSON along with the ZIP. The recipient can click Import
and choose that JSON. Version-20 setups include your account ascension ranks
and each team's trial builds. Ranks from version 19 become the account
baseline when you update. Use the latest HTML to import a version-20 setup.
