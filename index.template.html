How the comparison works Formulas & source

Experience compares boss XP received by the leveler, including XP shared by Veteran. Damage and Gold compare the attacker’s bonuses. Energy compares wave income, and Energy + Power Mages compares energy per Power Mage kill. These relative scores include configured styles, milestones, synergies, map, tiles and captured skills. Mimic farming weights Normal, Super and Ultra Gold by their chances. The legacy Gold index and other resource scores use guaranteed tiers. Chance references supply the chances for the selected attacker.

Milestones and synergies

Active heroes have updated milestones through level 10,000, unlocked at or below each hero’s level. Integer bases are inferred from rounded screenshot displays. Permanent milestone strength defaults to talent ×1.5 × global ×1.25 = ×1.875. Each owner gains 10% of that strength per Milestone Bonus level, up to +50%. A synergy activates when enough listed teammate copies are deployed and its tier is unlocked. Buying Synergy Heroes Required reduces the partner count by one for 3- and 4-hero synergies. The owner is always required. Independent percentage effects multiply; additive effects stay in a shared total. Synergy values are rounded to whole numbers after strength boosts; milestone values retain fractional precision. Style bonuses do not amplify themselves.

Account synergy bonuses

The enabled account profile multiplies three independent +5% global bonuses to ×1.157625, then applies ×6.25 to tiers 1–2 and ×2.5 to tiers 3–4 independently of map boosts. Each owner starts with a 0% Synergy Bonus. Each purchased level adds 5%, up to +25%. It scales effects emitted by that owner, including global effects, without stacking the owners into another global pool. An off-tile owner with a purchased +25% bonus and nine Grand Masters has 1.05³ × 1.25 × 1.1⁹ = 3.412023994742347 before tier factors. No extra calibration multiplier is applied.

Chance spillover

Mimic Gold checks Super first and then rolls Ultra on Super drops. Super overflow boosts both Super and Ultra amounts. Ultra overflow boosts Ultra amounts. The other objectives add each tier’s excess above 100% to its outcome’s shared spillover pool. Gold defaults to 50% Super and 50% Ultra before heroes; Damage defaults to 135% Crit, 109% Super and 48% Ultra before heroes. Pre-team mode adds candidate bonuses before checking the caps. Recorded-team mode uses observed total + candidate contribution − captured contribution. Each reference has its own Additional spillover value, replacing the shared existing-overflow input. At 5% per point, 20 excess points give one ×2 factor, not twenty separate ×1.05 multipliers. The already-capped assumption mode retains the earlier calculation explicitly. Mimic farming weights the three payout tiers by their probabilities. The legacy Gold index and Damage index assume guaranteed tiers.

Tycoon adds 5% global Rank EXP and synergy strength; Magnate adds 12% global Rank EXP and 8% synergy strength, so both can benefit Veteran-shared kills. Best available and the leveling plan compare them with the other unlocked styles. All copies of the leveling hero share its level and combat style. The Grand Master setting applies to unassigned support heroes. Experience supports can instead use Tycoon or Magnate, sharing their choice across copies. The style-search checkbox allows suggestions to compare those support styles; disabling it retains manual assignments. Locks protect heroes and levels while styles can change like tiles. The current comparison uses the displayed assignments. Two leveling copies leave at most eight Grand Masters on a full team and three leave at most seven; the bonus uses the actual count. Synergies separately receive account, owner, tier and map bonuses. The two stacking controls can be changed independently.
